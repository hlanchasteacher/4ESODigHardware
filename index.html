TechNova: Zero Budget Mission

Congratulations — you have just been hired as the new IT Purchasing Manager at TechNova, a small but ambitious creative agency.

Three employees have been working on machines that are now painfully slow, and the company has finally approved a hardware refresh. There is only one problem: the Finance Director has frozen the budget at €2,500 for all three workstations — not per workstation, in total. And she wants a written technical justification for every single euro you spend.

Your job is to research real components from real online stores, put together three builds, verify that every part is compatible, and defend your decisions like a professional. A wrong socket, the wrong RAM generation or an undersized power supply will mean a machine that simply does not turn on — and a very unhappy colleague.

€
Hard limit: €2,500.00 total for all three builds.

Going over budget is a failed mission. Leaving money unspent is also a problem — your colleagues need machines that actually do their job.

Mission objectives

  • Research real parts from real retailers and paste the product name and/or link.
  • Guarantee hardware compatibility: CPU socket ↔ motherboard socket, RAM generation, storage interface, power and form factor.
  • Keep the combined total at or under €2,500.
  • Justify every choice technically — "it was cheap" is not a justification.
  • Produce the final PDF report and upload it to Microsoft Teams.

How to work through this page

  1. Open a new browser tab with an online computer store and search for real components.
  2. For each profile, write the part name/link, its price, and a short technical justification.
  3. Complete the Compatibility check box at the end of every profile.
  4. Watch the budget bar at the top: it turns red the moment you exceed €2,500.
  5. Fill in the Reflection section at the bottom.
  6. Click “Generate Final Report”, check the preview and download your PDF.
